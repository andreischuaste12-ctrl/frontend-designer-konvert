# Example design brief

## Product context

- Product: fictional online stationery shop.
- Audience evidence: brief says visitors are first-time shoppers comparing notebooks; this is product-provided context, not independently researched.
- Primary task: choose a notebook and understand delivery cost before adding it to the cart.

## Reference and fidelity

- Source: `https://shop.example/[path-redacted]` (synthetic placeholder).
- Level: adaptation.
- Transferable idea: make size, paper type, and delivery information easy to compare near the purchase action.
- Do not copy: brand name, product photography, copy, or distinctive page composition.

## Scope and acceptance

- Scope: product detail page, desktop and mobile.
- Preserve: existing brand tokens, product data, and cart behavior.
- Accept when: selected options are clear, delivery details are visible before add-to-cart, keyboard focus is visible, and no horizontal overflow is present at the agreed viewports.

## Evidence labels

- Product facts: provided in the brief.
- Visual details: to be measured from the supplied reference if accessible.
- Any unmeasured style choices: mark as inferred or decided, never measured.

# Example audit report

> Illustrative sample only. No real website, customer, account, or user study was used.

## Context

- Page: fictional product detail page.
- Task: compare product options and add one item to cart.
- Audience: first-time shoppers, according to a synthetic product brief.
- Confidence: product brief only; no analytics or usability study available.

## Findings

| Priority | Observation | Likely effect | Confidence | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Delivery cost appears only after adding to cart. | Shoppers may postpone a decision or encounter an unexpected cost. | Observed in the supplied synthetic flow. | Show delivery estimate near price; verify shipping rules with product owner. |
| P2 | Option labels use internal paper codes without explanation. | New shoppers may not compare options confidently. | Observed; comprehension impact is a hypothesis. | Replace codes with plain-language attributes and test comprehension. |
| P3 | Secondary links compete visually with the purchase action. | The next step is less obvious. | Visual observation; behavior unmeasured. | Reduce secondary emphasis while preserving their function. |

## Validation

- Inspect the revised page at mobile and desktop widths.
- Test keyboard operation and visible focus.
- Ask representative users to compare two options and explain delivery cost.
- Do not claim conversion improvement without a suitable measurement plan.
